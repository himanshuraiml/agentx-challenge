Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We have answers. If you need further help, reach out to our community organizers.

Who is eligible to participate in the hackathon?

This is an internal hackathon exclusive to SRMIST Tiruchirappalli. Students from all technical departments are eligible and highly encouraged to participate. Teams can consist of 1 to 4 members.

What counts as an "Agentic AI" project?

An agentic system goes beyond simple LLM wrapping. Your submission should involve a reasoning architecture (e.g. ReAct, Plan-and-Solve, or custom loops), tool invocation capabilities (making web calls, executing code in sandbox, querying databases), and some degree of autonomy in solving a user task.

Can we submit projects that were built before?

No. All projects must be started and completed within the 24-hour hackathon build window (October 8 - 9, 2026). Pre-existing templates and basic open-source libraries are permitted, but code specific to the submission must be fresh.

How long is the build window, and how does judging work?

This is an intense 24-hour hackathon build. Submissions are subjected to 3 sequential rounds of judging: Round 1 (Technical & Code Depth), Round 2 (Agent Autonomy & Tool-Calling validation), and Round 3 (Live Finale Pitches by the top finalists).

Do we have to build in all five domains?

No. Your team should select one main focus domain (e.g., Healthcare or Agriculture) and build a deep, high-fidelity agentic solution for it. Submitting cross-domain projects is also fine as long as it has a clear primary focus.
